Hello Alpaca team,

We are evaluating a historical-only commercial research package for US ETF
trend following. The proposed scope is January 2, 2019 through September 1,
2026, inclusive, in America/New_York, for up to six instruments including
benchmark constituents. The design has 52 proposed monthly decisions, a
252-completed-session lookback and a 21-session holding horizon. The scope
is for pricing and technical review. Dates, instruments and exact windows
remain subject to approval. October 20 through December 31, 2026, inclusive,
America/New_York, is excluded.

Please route this to your historical data and licensing teams. We are
requesting written terms, field definitions, metadata-only coverage and
pricing. Please send no observations, samples, credentials or account
activation materials. This inquiry makes no purchase commitment.

- Can Historical Stock Bars (`timeframe=1Day`, `adjustment=raw`, `feed=sip`)
  and Historical Quotes (`feed=sip`) cover the proposed dates and regular
  sessions, including half days? Please confirm inactive symbols, mapping
  history, tape and venue scope, and entry and exit quote windows with enough
  surrounding context to establish eligible quote state and fill assumptions.

  publication, provider receipt and what was available to subscribers at a
  historical cutoff? Please specify quote timestamp origin, bid and ask sizes
  and units, conditions, venue identifiers and corrections or gaps.
- Which agreement permits historical-only commercial automated non-display
  research, benchmark and total-return calculations, raw and derived retention
  including backups, and named independent reviewer access? Please state
  allowed environments, reporting, redistribution and termination limits.
- Please quote first-year historical-only fees by product, venue, license,
  reviewer and minimum commitment, with quote expiry, renewal, cancellation
  and deletion obligations. Identify any required live plan separately.

Thank you.
