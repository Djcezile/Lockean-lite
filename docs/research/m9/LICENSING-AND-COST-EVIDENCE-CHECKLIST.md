# Licensing and cost evidence checklist

Record the named issuer, controlling written terms, version, dates and exact
scope for each product, exchange and sidecar. Match the evidence to the
frozen study and independent custody records.

- [ ] Exact legal product, plan, schema, issuer and agreement/version.
- [ ] Authorized commercial entity, user roles, environments and storage regions.
- [ ] Permitted historical dates, instruments, venues and archive depth.
- [ ] Automated offline commercial non-display processing and cross-provider joins.
- [ ] Derived features, exposure-matched benchmark and total-return calculations.
- [ ] Separate raw and derived retention, backups, security and post-termination rights.
- [ ] Named independent reviewer access and allowed access method.
- [ ] External reporting, publication and redistribution limits, including derived outputs.
- [ ] Original/corrected versions, corporate-action continuity and historical availability evidence.
- [ ] Binding itemized price, quote expiry, taxes, exchange and non-display fees,
  minimum commitments, reviewer charges, renewals, cancellation and deletion costs.

Record one-time provider charges, infrastructure and first-year recurring
commitments separately, counting each charge once. Check M8's planning
subcaps of USD 500 provider, USD 200 infrastructure and USD 300 recurring,
plus the USD 1,000 total ceiling. Include required base plans and expansions,
contract minimums and termination obligations. A known breach blocks even if
other amounts are absent. Missing amounts remain UNKNOWN. Public API access
or an individual plan does not establish commercial permission.

Only after evidence is reviewed should the Founder decide whether to
authorize a named package, period, budget and reviewer custody.
