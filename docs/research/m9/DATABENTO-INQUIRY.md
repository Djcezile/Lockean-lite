**Subject — Historical ETF Security Master and corporate action licensing**

Hello Databento team,

We are evaluating a historical-only commercial research package for US ETF
trend following. The proposed scope is January 2, 2019 through September 1,
2026, inclusive, in America/New_York, for up to six instruments including
benchmark constituents. The design has 52 proposed monthly decisions, a
252-completed-session lookback and a 21-session holding horizon. The scope
is for pricing and technical review. Dates, instruments and exact windows
remain subject to approval. October 20 through December 31, 2026, inclusive,
America/New_York, is excluded.

Please route this to your historical data and licensing teams. We are
requesting written terms, field definitions, metadata-only coverage and
pricing. Please send no observations, samples, credentials or account
activation materials. This inquiry makes no purchase commitment.

The prices and entry/exit quotes, including surrounding eligibility and fill
context, would come from a separately licensed Alpaca SIP package. This
request is for historical reference data and rights for the join.

- Can Security Master, Corporate Actions and Adjustment Factors cover the
  proposed dates, including inception, listings, closure and delisting,
  symbol and identifier continuity, ETF cash and special distributions and
  splits? Please specify listing-level keys and how original records,
  corrections and rescissions are linked and retrieved.
- Which fields identify first publication, provider receipt or availability
  and record-change time? Please distinguish effective dates and record
  timestamps from proof that a version could have been received by a
  historical subscriber. Identify missing vintages or uncertain coverage.
- Can a commercial research entity lawfully join these records to separately
  licensed Alpaca data for automated non-display research, benchmark and
  total-return calculations? Please specify the applicable agreements,
  raw and derived retention and backups, named independent reviewer access,
  and reporting, redistribution and post-termination restrictions.
- Please itemize historical-only charges, reference entitlements, source
  fees, reviewer access, minimum commitments, quote validity, renewals and
  cancellation/deletion costs. Separate any optional live products.

Thank you.
