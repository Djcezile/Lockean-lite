# M8 acquisition decision record

## BLOCKED

Start diligence with **Alpaca Market Data API Historical Stock Bars and
Historical Quotes, both SIP**, paired with **Databento Security Master and
Corporate Actions** and an Adjustment Factors reconciliation if quoted.
Massive Stocks Business is the full-market historical alternative.
Databento `EQUS.MINI` is a component-feed comparator and would need a
separately justified execution design before any substitution.

The ranking uses documented breadth, exact timestamp
and quote fields, the ability to scope historical access, and reference-data
coverage. The ranking is judgment about diligence priority. It does not
establish relative vendor quality, permissions or commercial feasibility.
Blockers are independently evidenced publication/receipt and original/corrected
versions, complete instrument and action continuity, exact commercial
non-display and reviewer/retention rights, and binding quotes for all cost
components. No candidate can clear these with public documentation alone.
USD 1,000 feasibility is inconclusive for a lawful historical-only package.
The published Full Market expansion scenario breaches that ceiling if required.

## Draft inquiry for Founder approval

**Recipients if approved** are the historical market-data licensing contact at
Alpaca, the Databento reference-data licensing contact, and Massive business
sales only if a comparison quote is wanted. This is an unsent request for
documentation and written pricing. It does not request trial credentials,
observations, an account or a contract commitment.

> We are evaluating a US ETF historical-only research license for up to six
> liquid unlevered funds or matched benchmark constituents. Please quote a
> fixed package for regular-session daily data from 2019-01-02 through
> 2026-09-01, and entry/exit quote windows around 52 preplanned monthly
> decisions and their 21-session exits. The symbol list would be frozen only
> after the coverage and cost terms are reviewed. Please provide:
>
> 1. Exact legal product, plan, schema versions and venue/tape scope. Confirm
>    historical coverage of raw daily OHLCV, separately split/distribution
>    adjusted views, quote sides, sizes, venue and eligibility fields and
>    effective instrument identifiers, including inactive and delisted funds.
> 2. Historical calendar/half-day treatment, bar interval and first publication
>    semantics, source and provider capture timestamps, availability evidence
>    available to a historical consumer, and original-to-corrected revision
>    archives. Describe known gaps, backfills and whether corrections can be
>    reconstructed as of a historical decision. Supply field definitions and
>    a metadata-only coverage statement. Please send no data samples.
> 3. Reference coverage for listing, inception, closure, delisting, cash and
>    special distributions, splits, symbol reuse and as-known revisions.
>    Identify event and record versions, effective times and historical
>    availability records. Document supported immutable export identifiers,
>    checksums and custody attestations.
> 4. Applicable signed terms or governing agreement version for a named
>    commercial research entity. Confirm automated offline non-display
>    processing, derived total returns/features/benchmark calculations, raw
>    and derived retention including backups and after termination, named
>    independent reviewer access, and permitted aggregate disclosures.
>    Itemize any limits on reproducibility, redistribution or publication.
> 5. Itemized one-time and first-year amounts, including minimum commitments,
>    per-schema/byte pricing, corporate actions and reference data, exchange
>    and non-display fees, reviewer seats, taxes, quote validity, renewal
>    notices, cancellation and deletion/exit costs. Quote historical-only
>    access separately from live plans and expansions.

Ask Alpaca specifically for the Historical Stock Bars and Historical Quotes
SIP endpoints and Corporate Actions SSE replay depth. Ask Databento for the
Security Master, Corporate Actions and Adjustment Factors reference histories
and permitted joins. Ask Massive whether Stocks Business can deliver the
historical SIP quote archive with a historical-only agreement and without a
live expansion.

## Founder decisions

1. Assign independent review at the exact M8 draft head. Confirm whether to
   send the inquiry and which entity and reviewer role can be named.
2. Approve the proposed dates, no more than six frozen instrument IDs or a
   revised scope, power and confirmation custody, and precise quote windows.
3. Resolve externally evidenced historical availability and revision custody.
   If unavailable, stop retrospective acquisition and propose a separately
   authorized prospective observation protocol.
4. Approve binding rights, named data environments, retention and all-in
   ceilings only after comparison. A later explicit acquisition decision must
   identify exact products, instruments, allowed dates, costs and custody.
5. Keep earnings deferred until its separate estimates, release, mappings,
   rights, evidence and quote package can be reviewed.
