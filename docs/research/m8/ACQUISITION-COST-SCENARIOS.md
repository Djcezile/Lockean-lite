# M8 acquisition cost scenarios

As of 2026-11-06 the verified cost of a lawful package is **UNKNOWN** for
every scenario. The USD 1,000 ceiling is a planning constraint with M7
subcaps of USD 500 provider, USD 200 infrastructure and USD 300 first-year
recurring commitments. These figures are limits, not estimates or spending
authority.

For a quote-sizing scenario of up to six frozen instruments including any
benchmark constituents, budget for up to 11,562 daily instrument-session
records (6 × 1,927) plus historical mapping, corporate actions, revisions and
publication/receipt evidence. Budget for up to 624 entry/exit instrument
quote windows (6 × 52 × 2), with a prior quote state and sufficient depth
around each window. Counts are scope bounds, not a cost or a promise of
volume. Ask for exact billed bytes, minimum charges and any additional
benchmark feeds. One venue archive may price whole days even when the
study needs a narrow slice.

Record the exact quote validity, committed months, renewal notice, taxes,
venue fees, retention/exit obligations, termination charges and control of
derived artifacts after termination. Classify each charge once as one-time
provider, infrastructure, or first-year recurring. Compute total as the sum
of all three and compare each subcap and the total. A known breach blocks
even when other amounts are unknown. Missing amounts cannot be treated as
zero. Preserve the applicable cost model for future all-in economics without
using these planning figures as strategy returns or executable costs.
