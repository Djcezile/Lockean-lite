# M7 cost and budget gates

All provider quotes and exact rights are UNKNOWN. The suggested ceiling is
**USD 1,000 for a future trend feasibility study**, subject to Founder approval.
It is an engineering limit for diligence and a small offline study, not a
vendor price or authority to spend. Proposed subcaps are USD 500 for
historical provider and applicable fees, USD 200 for infrastructure, and
USD 300 for recurring commitments within the first study year. Each amount
includes applicable taxes and unavoidable charges in its category. Quotes
must satisfy each cap and the aggregate cap. No earnings budget is proposed
for activation. A separate itemized estimate and Founder decision are required
if the additional consensus/release products cannot fit an approved envelope.

| Ledger category | Trend emphasis | Earnings emphasis |
|---|---|---|
| Historical acquisition and commercial rights | ETF daily bars, timestamped quotes, security history, distributions, venue and non-display fees | Equity bars/quotes, point-in-time listing history, original EPS, consensus vintages, first release and receipt archives |
| Infrastructure | Data volume, encrypted storage/backups, parsing, replay compute, retention and audit | Cross-provider issuer/security joins, revision storage, clock reconciliation and event QA |
| Recurring expense | Renewals, reviewer seats/access, storage, cloud, monitoring, maintenance and engineering time | Separate estimates/release subscriptions, data curation and mapping maintenance |
| Variable execution and carrying costs | Bid/ask, impact, commissions, slippage, missed fills, turnover, cash carry and capital use | Gaps, correlated releases, spreads, capacity, fees and impact. Borrow, recalls and financing if shorting is separately approved. |

Freeze study horizon, cost allocation, expected traded capital, available
capacity, drawdown cap and quotes before access. At the horizon level, the
minimum required net advantage in dollars is allocated fixed and recurring
study/operating expense plus the approved capital charge and an uncertainty
margin. Divide by the same deployed-capital basis as the incremental return
contrast to express the hurdle in basis points. Require the after-variable-cost
effect to exceed both that break-even hurdle and a preregistered minimum effect.
For example, a proposed 10 bp residual margin is a decision threshold, not a
strategy return. M7 fixture inputs use 10 bp minimum effect, 1,000 bp drawdown
limit, USD 1,000 capacity and illustrative spread/slippage/fee assumptions of
5/5/1 bp. They have no empirical calibration or risk-policy effect.

Use executable quote sides, spread and modeled impact only once in the cost
bridge. Report a base and adverse scenario with widened spreads, latency,
partial/no fills, increased turnover, smaller usable capacity, and recurring
costs and financing where relevant. Keep statistical predictability, tradable
after-cost results, broker-level paper P&L and all-in business economics separate.
Reject or defer for unknown or expired quotes, cost-cap breaches, unauthenticated
rights, implausible fill/capacity assumptions or failure of the economic or
drawdown gates. Neither capital nor infrastructure can be committed under M7.
