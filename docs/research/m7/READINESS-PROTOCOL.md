## Priority 1 · ETF trend

The hypothesis is that a positive trailing 252-completed-session total return
adds to the next 21-session return, compared with an exposure-matched passive
allocation. The null is a nonpositive paired incremental effect. Freeze a
small universe of liquid, unlevered ETFs and a prior-observable liquidity
screen before any data access. Use a stable security identifier, original
listings and delistings, and distributions and splits as known. Freeze
eligibility and the monthly schedule. Use equal-notional long/cash allocation
and no volatility scaling in the primary comparison. Cash and buy-and-hold
are descriptive comparisons. Match passive exposure and cash carry and
evaluate whether passive beta explains the result.

Use completed daily bars through the prior eligible session. Issue a decision
only after publication and verified local receipt. Enter at a later eligible
tradable window and model both legs using executable quote sides, size,
slippage, fees and missed or partial fills. Report capital use, drawdown,
turnover and concentration. Rapid trend reversals and repeated crossings are
adverse regimes to predeclare. A null or cost-sensitive result fails the
progression gate.

## Priority 2 · conditional earnings drift

The hypothesis is that a positive as-issued EPS surprise adds to the next
20-session sector- and liquidity-matched return. The null is a nonpositive
paired incremental effect. Use a fixed, predeclared liquid US common-share
universe and long/cash initially. The prospective surprise is original
comparable EPS minus the latest independently evidenced, eligible pre-release
consensus for the same issuer, measure, currency, basis and fiscal period.
The primary classifier is strictly positive surprise. Match against eligible
nonsurprise releases, with passive same-risk exposure as a descriptive control.
Freeze matching and outlier rules before access.

Require original issuer release and revision identity, authenticated first
publication, provider and local receipt, archived pre-release consensus
vintage, and fiscal/security mapping. Apply a next-eligible-session cutoff
to after-hours and pre-open releases. Skip an event if the cutoff, clocks or
as-issued record cannot be established. Later revisions and reconstructed
consensus cannot substitute for the original. Model gaps, spreads, fill risk
and clustered impact. Failure to authenticate EPS, consensus, receipt or
rights blocks real-data admission regardless of historical literature.

## Statistical and chronological freeze

Record exact inputs, universe, calendar, development/validation/holdout dates,
lookbacks, execution schedule, allocation, endpoint and economic hurdles before
access. Use chronological expanding windows in development, followed by
validation and a single independently held confirmation. Separate dependency
spans include features, lookbacks, benchmarks, execution and label/holding
windows. Purge overlap and embargo at least the maximum label horizon in
eligible sessions. Never change a locked rule using holdout feedback.
The protected period is October 20 through December 31, 2026, inclusive,
America/New_York. Reject any dependency span that overlaps or straddles it.

Use one paired one-sided primary contrast per candidate. Preregister the
sample size, power and dependence-aware intervals, using date blocks for ETFs
and date/issuer clustering or justified block resampling for earnings.
Apply Holm at familywise alpha 0.05 across all confirmatory candidates and
variants. Record every trial and preserve a fresh confirmation window if a
post-validation change is separately authorized. Require the statistical
contrast and the predeclared after-cost and capital gates. Insufficient
effective sample size is inconclusive. Unauthenticated data, rights gaps,
leakage, clock ambiguity and protocol drift invalidate the study.

Independent review requires exact code and protocol digests, externally
authenticated custody and rights, complete trial and cost ledgers, and
reproduction of timing, purging and benchmark controls. Review eligibility
precedes approval. Empirical execution and any operational action require
separate authority.
