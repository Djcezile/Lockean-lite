## Reasons and disqualifiers

- **Trend-following momentum.** A scheduled ETF study can test a clear
  continuation hypothesis with fewer availability joins and more time for
  independent approval. The supporting futures evidence is indirect. Drop the
  candidate if the test cannot distinguish incremental value from passive
  exposure or volatility scaling, if usable as-known ETF histories or quotes
  cannot be secured, or if stress and capital limits fail. Moderate confidence
  in research priority, with current edge and cost UNKNOWN.
- **Post-earnings announcement drift.** Earnings releases provide a discrete
  event and falsifiable surprise mechanism. Historical drift is conditional on
  how surprise and liquidity are measured. Drop or defer the candidate if
  comparable as-released EPS and pre-release consensus cannot be authenticated,
  the liquid universe has inadequate effective sample size, rights or cost
  gates fail, or plausible gap/slippage erases the edge. Moderate confidence
  in research priority, with current edge and cost UNKNOWN.
- **Intraday ETF momentum.** First alternate under the balanced framework.
  Reconsider if executable pre-close timing, quote depth and cost evidence
  becomes stronger than the event-data case.
- **Short-term mean reversion.** Reconsider with evidence that reversal survives
  stale-quote controls and liquid, executable fills at the intended size.
- **News-reaction trading.** Defer until timestamped revision history and usable
  rights are proved, and feasible authorization latency leaves a testable window.
