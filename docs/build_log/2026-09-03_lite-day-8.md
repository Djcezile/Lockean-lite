## Hidden Post-Agent Gate Discovered

Live execution preparation exposed a remaining architectural inconsistency.

Although the AI was no longer gated before recommendation, `evidence_validation.py` still reapplied `evaluate_market_entry_policy()` after a TRADE recommendation.

That meant failed indicators could still function as execution permission rules downstream of the AI.

The responsibility boundary was corrected.

Evidence validation now verifies only:

- proposal/evidence symbol identity
- trusted evidence sources
- shared `as_of`
- internal timestamp consistency
- exact proposal fingerprint binding

Market-entry indicators remain agent context and reporting information.

They are no longer authorization rules.

## Architectural Separation of Powers

The resulting workflow enforces three intentionally incomplete authorities:

### AI Agent

- may evaluate market evidence
- may decide TRADE or NO_TRADE
- may propose a trade
- cannot authorize itself
- cannot issue Authorization Receipts
- cannot submit broker orders

### Lockean Authority

- independently validates trusted evidence
- independently calculates trusted risk
- enforces account and policy constraints
- may issue a short-lived proposal-bound Authorization Receipt
- cannot invent the agent's trade
- does not possess broker execution authority

### Execution Gateway

- possesses broker access
- independently verifies the Authorization Receipt
- verifies the receipt against the exact proposal fingerprint
- cannot create authorization
- cannot self-authorize a changed proposal

Summary:

> The AI provides judgment without execution power. Lockean provides authorization without broker authority. The Execution Gateway provides broker access without the ability to self-authorize.
