reconciled without unknown orders, API-stage failures, tracebacks, or excess

## Strategy Finding

The active-paper prompt generated 29 consecutive trade judgments once evidence
became available. Thirteen filled spreads subsequently reached the stop-loss
path, while no profit-taking order filled. The portfolio and exact-structure
caps limited simultaneous exposure but did not limit repeated same-direction
entries after realized losses.

This is not evidence that the stop-loss engine failed. It is evidence that the
entry policy lacks a session-level loss-loop control and that `active_paper` is
unsuitable for continued strategy evaluation without containment. Day 14 does
not justify changing profit or loss thresholds from one session of data.

## Minimum Safe Follow-up

An opt-in `--risk-management-only` session mode was added. It:

- preserves broker reconciliation and existing-position exit checks;
- preserves take-profit and stop-loss execution authority;
- disables every new AI entry cycle;
- cancels any pending entry MLEG before acting on a stale portfolio snapshot;
- leaves the default session behavior unchanged when the flag is absent.

a consecutive-loss circuit breaker, a maximum filled-entry count per session,
or a combination of those controls. That strategy/risk choice must be evaluated
from accumulated evidence rather than silently embedded in the lifecycle fix.
