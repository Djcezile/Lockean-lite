# Lockean Lite — Day 16 Loss-Loop Validation

**Date:** September 29, 2026

**Mode:** Alpaca paper, controlled active entries

**Purpose:** Validate the deterministic loss-loop policy added after Day 15:
the first confirmed stop-loss fill blocks that direction for 60 minutes, and
the second confirmed stop-loss spread-unit fill halts all new entries for the
remainder of the market session.

## Outcome

Day 16 completed automatically after the Alpaca market closed. The loss-loop
control passed every live acceptance criterion that the session exercised.
The runner submitted two entries, closed both through stop-loss orders, rejected
one same-direction proposal after the first stop, and blocked every later entry
cycle after the second stop. It finished flat with no pending or unknown orders.

One Alpaca session-state read timed out after the hard halt was active. The
runner failed closed, made no autonomous order attempt, recovered on the next
loop, and retained the broker-reconstructed halt. No code defect or unsafe
state transition was observed.

## Policy Timeline

1. A bullish call spread filled and stopped. Broker history then reconstructed
   one bullish stop-loss fill and activated the 3,600-second bullish block.
2. The next bullish proposal was rejected by deterministic policy before any
   broker order was submitted.
3. A later bearish put-spread proposal remained eligible because the first-stop
   restriction was directional. It filled and stopped.
4. Broker history reconstructed two stop-loss spread-unit fills and activated
   the session-wide entry halt.
5. Every later eligible entry cycle was blocked while reconciliation, position
   checks, end-of-day cleanup, and market-close termination continued.

Day 16 proves that the new control prevents the repeated post-stop entry loop
seen on Day 14. It does not prove that the entry strategy is profitable; both
Day 16 entries lost money, and one controlled session is too small a sample for
threshold tuning.

## Decision

No code or threshold change is justified by this log. Day 17 should repeat the
same active-paper configuration to collect another independent session while
preserving the now-live-proven containment. The 60-minute directional cooldown,
two-stop session halt, 20% take-profit, and 20% stop-loss remain unchanged.

The pending-entry cancellation path was not exercised because no entry order
was working when either restriction activated. Restart reconstruction and that
cancellation path remain regression-test proven rather than live-session proven.
