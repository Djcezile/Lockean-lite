# Lockean Lite — Day 15 Risk-Only Session Evidence

**Date:** September 28, 2026

**Mode:** Alpaca paper, risk management only

**Purpose:** Manage the two spreads inherited from Day 14 to a terminal broker
state without authorizing any new exposure.

## Outcome

Day 15 completed automatically after the Alpaca market closed. Risk-only
containment passed: the runner executed no AI entry cycles, submitted no entry
orders, and kept the normal reconciliation and position-exit path active. Both
inherited defined-risk SPY debit call spreads closed through stop-loss exits.
The final broker snapshot was flat, with no positions and no pending or unknown
orders.

Five stop-loss submissions represented two position exits rather than five
separate positions. One spread filled on its first order. The other required
three stale-order cancel/reconcile cycles before its fourth submission filled.

The first spread closed at a $0.43 credit. The second spread's exit was

and $0.35. The final cash movement shows a $0.37 fill, two cents better than
the last limit. Combined closing credits were $0.80 against $1.15 of original
entry debit, a $0.35 gross lifecycle loss before commissions. Part of that loss
was already reflected in Day 14's closing unrealized P&L, so the Day 15 account
change was $23.10.

## Proven Behavior

- `--risk-management-only` disabled every new AI entry cycle and entry order.
- Broker reconciliation and the normal 30-second position checks remained
  active while entry authority was disabled.
- Stop-loss authority remained active and closed both inherited spreads.
- Stale exit orders were cancelled only after reconciliation and were safely
  resubmitted without duplicate exposure or an unknown-order state.
- The session reached a clean market-close terminal state with cash equal to
  equity and every position and order count at zero.
- No component crossed the proposal, authorization, and execution boundaries.

## Execution-Quality Finding

The lifecycle behaved correctly, but the second stop-loss exit took three
timeout/reprice cycles while its quoted executable credit moved from $0.42 to
$0.35. The eventual $0.37 fill recovered two cents of that movement. This is
useful evidence that the current passive timeout/repricing policy can delay a
risk exit in a moving market; it is not, by itself, sufficient evidence to
change timeout or pricing policy.

## Decision Gate

The Day 14 exposure has been fully contained, so another flat risk-only session
would add little evidence. Active entries remain paused until a deterministic
session-level loss-loop policy is selected and tested. The preferred next
control should combine a same-direction cooldown after the first stop loss with
a hard session halt after a small consecutive-loss limit. Exact thresholds are
a strategy/risk decision and are intentionally not changed by this evidence
record.

Live-capital authority remains disabled. No Lockean Lite code is bulk-transferred
into Project Lockean Core.
