## Diagnostic Finding

The system behaved safely, but it classified an expected official-data publication delay as a generic autonomous-cycle error. That made a controlled entry-evidence wait look like a broader runtime failure.

The follow-up change introduces `ENTRY_EVIDENCE_WAIT` for three recognized transient evidence conditions:

- `spy_completed_session_missing`
- `vix_completed_session_missing`
- `vix_evidence_unavailable`

These conditions continue to block new entries and retry on the normal entry cadence. All other model, broker, validation, and programming exceptions retain the existing `CYCLE_ERROR` path.

**Regression result:** 320 passed, 1 known third-party deprecation warning, 0 regressions.

## Remaining Boundary

No stale VIX fallback was introduced. Lockean still requires completed-session evidence for the configured date and will not fabricate bars, silently shift dates, or substitute a different volatility product. Any future availability fallback must preserve source provenance, same-date evidence, and the separation between judgment, authorization, and execution.

Day 13 proves fail-closed evidence handling and clean session reconciliation. It does not provide strategy-performance evidence or support live-capital activation.
