## What Day 19 Proved

1. The Day 18 preflight correction worked in production. Under an ineligible
   market, AI inference fell from 24 requests on Day 18 to zero on Day 19.
2. The launcher created the log inside the repository's `logs` subfolder and
   recorded an on-time premarket start.
3. The runtime remained stable through premarket, the full regular session,
   the end-of-day cutoff, scorecard output, and automatic shutdown.
4. Portfolio, loss-loop, pending-order, and exit monitoring continued without
   creating exposure.
5. Capital was preserved inside the broker account for this session.

## What Day 19 Did Not Prove

1. It did not produce positive broker P&L or positive all-in P&L.
2. It did not validate or falsify the bullish-confluence hypothesis because no
   market observation passed all prerequisites.
3. It did not test option selection, trusted debit, the 1.50:1 after-cost
   reward-to-risk threshold, entry fills, exits, cooldown, or stop containment.
4. It added zero closed trades toward the 100-trade, 20-session promotion
   minimum and supplied no new expectancy, profit-factor, or drawdown sample.
5. It did not establish the strategy's opportunity frequency. Two flat,
   ineligible sessions are far too small a sample.

Day 19 does disprove one operational concern: the new preflight was not merely
a unit-tested optimization; it actually prevented all unnecessary AI requests
in a full live paper session. It does not disprove the strategy's edge.

## Evidence-Required Correction

The first 09:30 evaluation established an immutable completed-session failure,
but the runner reloaded and re-evaluated the same production market evidence 23
more times. That did not spend AI requests, but it still performed work that
could not change the session's entry eligibility.

The post-session correction now:

1. latches a failed completed-session trend, momentum, breakout, or volatility
   result for the rest of the run;
2. blocks later 15-minute entry cycles from repeating production evidence and
   option/AI work;
3. keeps every 30-second portfolio, pending-order, loss-loop, and exit check
   active;
4. counts AI inference requests explicitly, including a request that raises an
   exception before returning; and
5. renames `AI ENTRY CADENCE` to `ENTRY EVALUATION CADENCE`, because an entry
   evaluation no longer implies an AI request.

No trend, momentum, breakout, volatility, alignment, economics, sizing, loss,
or exit threshold was relaxed. Changing any of those would be a fundamental
strategy change without counterfactual P&L evidence.

## Current Path

Two tracks now run in parallel conceptually, without mixing evidence:

1. **Forward paper validation:** Day 20 runs the unchanged hypothesis with the
   corrected session latch and inference accounting. It can trade
   automatically only if every deterministic market, intraday, structure,
   economics, portfolio, authority, and execution check passes.
2. **Historical counterfactual research:** build a reproducible dataset of
   completed SPY/VIX signals, intraday alignment, contemporaneous option quotes
   or defensible spread-price proxies, and rule-faithful exits. Use it to
   measure opportunity count, after-cost expectancy, profit factor, drawdown,
   concentration, and held-out performance before proposing any parameter or
   directional expansion.

The next fundamental decision is evidence-gated: retain, revise, or retire the
bullish-confluence hypothesis only after counterfactual results exist. The live
runner must not be loosened simply to manufacture trade count.
