# Lockean Lite — Autonomous Engineering Contract

## 1. Purpose and authority

Work only in public Djcezile/Lockean-lite under a Founder-authorized milestone.

Before editing, record:
- Approved objective, baseline and acceptance criteria.
- Owned components/directories and protected components.
- Behavioral, data and resource boundaries.
- Whether PR publication is authorized.

This contract governs execution of approved work; it does not authorize new
milestones, purchases, data access, trading, merge or deployment.

## 2. Autonomous work within an approved milestone

Within approved component ownership, Codex may:
- Plan and implement the milestone.
- Write failing tests before runtime corrections or new behavior.
- Add necessary in-scope tests, synthetic fixtures and documentation.
- Diagnose and correct routine defects.
- Run targeted and relevant regressions.
- Preserve evidence and prepare a consolidated handoff.
- Commit, push and prepare one reviewable PR when publication is authorized.

Component ownership is bounded by responsibility, not an unrestricted directory
write grant. Explicit protected files and narrower milestone restrictions remain
binding. Do not refactor unrelated components.

Do not request repeated Founder approval for routine debugging, implementation
choices or in-scope regression additions.

## 3. Worktree and repository isolation

Verify origin, baseline, branch and working-tree status before changing files.
Use an isolated worktree. Preserve unrelated changes and historical evidence.

Do not use or alter the operational Windows checkout or scheduled PAPER tasks
for engineering work.

Do not access private Original Lockean, transfer its code, or copy Lite changes
into it. Stop if an assignment requires crossing that boundary.

## 4. TDD and verification

For runtime changes:
1. Capture a genuine failing regression before implementation.
2. Implement the minimum compliant correction.
3. Run focused tests, applicable existing regressions and the full suite.
4. Check the final diff, protected components and source identities.

Distinguish newly failing tests from already-passing regression additions.
For documentation-only changes, source/test traceability and diff checks are
normally sufficient; execute tests only to resolve a concrete contradiction.

Preserve immutable RED and GREEN evidence:
- Exact commands and working directories.
- Actual exit codes, output, test counts, warnings and skips.
- Source revision and relevant input/schema identities.

Never weaken tests, suppress failures, remove integrity checks or change the
approved contract merely to obtain GREEN. Any justified test correction must
trace to the approved contract and retain the original failure evidence.

Do not treat zero collected tests, unexplained omissions or unexpected skips
as success. Distinguish historical results from newly executed verification.

## 5. Research integrity

Preserve chronology, completed-observation timing, provenance, approved purpose,
calendar semantics, uncertainty and immutable evidence.

Bind deterministic artifacts to versioned schemas, source identities, approved
inputs and relevant policies. Do not overwrite old datasets, reports or logs.
Hashes establish consistency; they do not alone authenticate provenance,
availability, licensing or approval.

Do not silently migrate existing canonical schemas or reinterpret old hashes.
Resolve typed-parameter identity before parameters influence evaluation.

Never represent synthetic qualification, admitted metadata, UNKNOWN evidence or
SPY proxies as authenticated historical evidence, executable options returns or
established profitability.

## 6. Protected research period

Do not read, retrieve, summarize or use actual October 20–December 31, 2026
protected observations or outcomes for hypothesis development, tuning or
selection.

General engineering, evidence-admission or collection authorization does not
override this prohibition. Any future confirmatory allocation requires a
separate reviewed protocol and explicit Founder authorization; this contract
does not grant access.

Generated counterfactual fixtures may use boundary timestamps to test exclusion.
They must never be represented as actual protected observations.

## 7. Independent review

Chronology, holdout, provenance, approval trust, canonical identity, financial
and security-sensitive changes require independent review by Sentinel or an
explicitly assigned reviewer.

The reviewer must inspect the actual diff at the proposed commit, the affected
trust and authorization boundaries, and the critical tests supporting the claims.
Where appropriate, independently execute critical tests or reproduce the relevant
failure and correction. State platform/access limitations and unresolved evidence
gaps explicitly; do not approve sensitive claims without sufficient verification.
Review is not satisfied by accepting an implementation summary or test counts alone.

The implementer's tests and self-review are preliminary evidence, not independent
acceptance. Review must bind to the exact proposed commit. Material changes after
review require review of the changed revision.

## 8. Separate authorization domains

Engineering implementation does not imply:
- PR publication.
- Merge.
- Operational deployment or scheduler activation.
- Broker/account activity, including read-only authenticated requests.
- Financial expenditures or paid services.
- Trading strategy, risk-policy or financial-authority changes.

Each domain requires explicit Founder authorization. Existing authorization
persists for its stated scope; do not request it repeatedly.

Merge additionally requires independent review and verified GitHub requirements.
Never override protections or use engineering approval as merge authority.

Preserve immutable proposals, independent Authority, proposal-bound receipts and
independent Execution Gateway boundaries.

## 9. Stop conditions

Investigate ordinary failures autonomously within scope. Stop with preserved
evidence and the smallest proposed decision when:
- A required change crosses component ownership or protected behavior.
- Passing a gate would require weakening safeguards or changing the contract.
- An unexpected regression remains unresolved or requires out-of-scope repairs.
- Required trust, provenance, rights or approval evidence is insufficient for
  the proposed action.
- Work requires unauthorized broker access, credentials, protected data,
  acquisition, purchases, deployment or Original Lockean access.
- Resource use would materially exceed the approved budget.

Do not claim completion while a mandatory gate remains unresolved.
Expected UNKNOWN research evidence may be a correct result; it never authorizes
downstream use requiring that evidence.

## 10. Evidence and handoff

Produce one consolidated milestone journal/handoff and a SHA-256 manifest.
Include:
- Timestamp and timezone, baseline and final revision.
- Scope, changed components and source-integrity checks.
- RED/GREEN evidence, actual commands/results and limitations.
- Artifact paths, hashes and review/publication status.
- Outstanding decisions and one next action.

Require sanitized evidence and redacted diagnostics. Do not publish secrets,
tokens, credentials, confidential information or restricted provider material in
logs, manifests, PRs or handoffs. Preserve reproducibility using safe metadata and
hashes without disclosing protected contents. Capture evidence safely from the
outset; retain immutable sanitized RED/GREEN records without retaining prohibited
contents in those artifacts.

Keep artifacts outside Git unless their tracked locations are approved.
Preserve earlier evidence rather than replacing it.
Do not create administrative micro-milestones or new orchestration infrastructure
unless essential to an approved requirement.

If publication fails, report the exact blocker and retain the reviewable branch
and PR description. Never claim a PR was created when it was not.

## 11. Model and resource escalation

Use GPT-6 Medium by default. Use High for complex trust, chronology, canonical
identity, security or financial verification.

Escalate reasoning within the approved resource budget when a concrete unresolved
risk warrants it. Escalate scope, expenditures or authorization to the Founder.
More reasoning does not supply missing authority or external evidence.

Stop optional testing once the required gates and concrete risks are resolved.

## 12. Research-admission safeguards

Research admission must separate trusted approval context from the untrusted
submitted evidence package. The package cannot supply or replace its own trusted
anchor. Approval issuance and the authenticity of external evidence must be
independently established, not inferred from matching self-supplied hashes.

Until a separately reviewed scope authorizes real-data work, admission development
and fixtures are synthetic-only. Where an admission contract uses these outcomes:
- ADMITTED: all required admission checks pass for the declared approved purpose
  against the independently pinned trust context; in synthetic work, this is only
  synthetic metadata admission.
- UNKNOWN: required evidence is missing or unverified; admission is withheld.
- REJECTED: malformed, inconsistent, tampered, unauthorized or untrusted evidence.

REJECTED takes precedence over UNKNOWN, then ADMITTED. Keep admission status
distinct from existing dataset-qualification and workflow statuses; do not rename
or reinterpret existing schemas through this instruction.

ADMITTED alone does not authenticate a real provider, approve protected-data
access, authorize collection or trading, establish historical options availability,
or prove profit. No automatic downstream research or operational action follows
admission. Real-data provenance, historical availability, licensing and retention
rights require sufficient independent evidence for the intended use.
